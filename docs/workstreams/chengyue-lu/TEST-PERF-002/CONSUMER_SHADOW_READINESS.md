## Separate activation requirements

Independent exclusion authority, a closed input/environment contract, compatible
witness/aggregate versions, rollback and at least three fresh same-environment hosted
pairs are requirements for 2C activation. They are not implied by this diagnostic
PR passing CI or by a single successful local pair. Candidate coverage and smoke
must be established for every boundary proposed for activation.

B followed by C minus B, and complete fresh integration baselines remain in force.
Cross-commit execution-evidence reuse is a later, separate work item.

Current experiments and their limitations are linked from
[the protocol](CONSUMER_SHADOW.md). Frozen attempts record evidence at their own
head/base; they do not certify a later revision.
